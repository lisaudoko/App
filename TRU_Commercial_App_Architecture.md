# Throwers R Us App — Commercial Architecture Design
**Scaling from One Coach to Many | NexGen Optimize**
**Version 1.0 | July 24, 2026**

---

## The Core Idea

Right now we are building for Coach Vassell. But if we design it correctly from the beginning, the same app becomes a platform any throws coach — or any strength and conditioning coach — can use with their own athletes, their own training system, and their own style.

The key is to build the app so that Coach Vassell's specific setup (his events, his lift selection, his mesocycle structure) is just one *configuration* of a flexible system, not the system itself.

---

## Part 1: Add Athlete — Functionality Categories

These are the categories of information the Add Athlete form will collect. The actual fields within each category can be customized per coach (see Part 3).

---

### Category 1: Identity
Who this athlete is.

- Full name
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Profile photo (optional)
- Athlete ID (auto-generated by the system)

---

### Category 2: Programme Placement
Where this athlete belongs within the coach's programme.

- Assigned coach (for multi-coach programmes)
- Team / Group (coach defines their own team names — e.g., "UTech," "HS Seniors," "Group A")
- Competitive class / category (e.g., Class 1, U20, Open, Masters — coach defines these)
- Start date (when the athlete joined the programme)

---

### Category 3: Event Profile
What this athlete competes in.

- Primary competitive event (from the coach's event list)
- Secondary competitive event (optional)
- Athlete role (e.g., Thrower, Sprinter, Jumper — if the coach works across disciplines)

---

### Category 4: Physical Baseline
Starting numbers captured at the beginning of the season. These become the reference point for measuring progress.

- Baseline competitive mark (in the unit the coach uses — metres, feet, seconds, etc.)
- Personal Best (all-time)
- Season Best (current season)
- Baseline lift maxes — populated from whichever lifts the coach has configured (see Part 3)

---

### Category 5: Status & Availability
Current training status.

- Active
- Injured (with optional return date and injury note)
- Inactive / Departed (archived — never deleted)
- On Rest / Recovery

---

### Category 6: Contact Information (Optional)
Useful for minor athletes or communication.

- Parent / guardian name (for athletes under 18)
- Emergency contact phone
- Email (for login credentials if the athlete will use the app)

---

### Category 7: Custom Fields (Coach-Defined)
Any additional information the coach wants to track that doesn't fit the standard categories. The coach creates their own labels.

Examples:
- "Training Age (years)"
- "School / Institution"
- "IAAF World Ranking"
- "Nutritional plan on file? (Y/N)"

## Part 2: Multi-Coach / Multi-Programme Architecture

For the app to scale commercially, it needs to support the concept of separate, isolated coaching programmes all running inside the same platform.

---

### The Programme Layer

Every coach who signs up creates a **Programme** — their own private workspace inside the app. Think of it like how each store on Shopify is separate even though they all run on the same platform.

Each Programme has:
- A unique programme name (e.g., "Throwers R Us," "Kingston AC Throws," "UTech Throws")
- Its own athlete roster — completely private, invisible to other coaches
- Its own configuration (events, lift list, periodization structure, group names)
- Its own performance data
- Its own coach account(s)

Coaches never see each other's athletes or data. Everything is isolated.

---

### User Roles Within a Programme

| Role | What They Can Do |
|---|---|
| Head Coach (Admin) | Full access — configure programme, manage athletes, view all data, invite assistant coaches |
| Assistant Coach | View and manage athletes, log and edit results, cannot change programme settings |
| Athlete | Log their own results, view their own workout and report only |

---

### Multi-Coach Inside One Programme

A university or club might have one Head Coach and two or three assistants. The Head Coach invites assistants, who can be assigned to specific groups or athletes. This is important for the Jamaica coaching network — universities like UTech or UWI might want this.

## Part 3: Configurable Training System

This is where the commercial power lives. Instead of hardcoding Coach Vassell's exact training methodology, every component is configurable so coaches with different philosophies can set up the system their way.

---

### 3.1 Event Library (Coach-Configured)

Coach Vassell uses: Shot Put, Discus, Javelin, Hammer.

Another coach might use: 100m, 200m, 400m, Long Jump.
A powerlifting coach might use: Squat, Bench Press, Deadlift (as the "event").

The coach defines their own event list. The app tracks results in whatever units apply (metres, seconds, kg, lbs).

---

### 3.2 Lift / Metric Library (Coach-Configured)

Coach Vassell tracks: Wide Bench Press, Back Press, Squats, Cleans, Snatch, Deadlifts, and derived lifts.

Another coach might only track: Back Squat, Bench Press, Power Clean.
A sprints coach might track: 30m fly time, vertical jump height, hip thrust max.

The coach adds exactly the lifts and metrics they want to track. The system builds athlete baselines, maxes, and reports from whatever the coach has configured.

---

### 3.3 Periodization Template (Coach-Configured)

Coach Vassell uses a 52-week season running September to August with these phases: GPP, SPP, Pre-Competition, Competition, Peak, Nationals, Transition, Recovery.

Another coach might use:
- A 36-week season
- Only four phases: Base, Build, Peak, Recovery
- Different phase names

The coach sets their own season start date, season length, phase names, and phase durations. The calendar auto-populates from there.

---

### 3.4 Prescription Engine (Coach-Configured)

Coach Vassell's mesocycle uses 4 weeks with these intensity percentages: 90%, 85%, 82%, 95%.

Another coach might use:
- 3-week waves: 75%, 80%, 85%
- 5-week waves with different rep schemes
- A completely different prescription logic

The coach configures:
- Number of weeks per mesocycle (2, 3, 4, 5, or 6)
- Intensity % per week
- Rep scheme per week
- Set pyramid structure (or flat sets, coach's choice)
- Derived lift ratios (or turn derived lifts off entirely)
- Number of lift days per week

The workout generator then calculates per-athlete weights automatically based on whatever the coach has configured.

---

### 3.5 Group / Team Structure (Coach-Configured)

Coach Vassell has: HS Juniors, HS Seniors, Seniors (UTech, Throwers R Us, club).

Another coach might have: Men's Team, Women's Team.
A school coach might have: Year 10, Year 11, Year 12.

Coach defines their own group names. Athletes are assigned to groups. Weekly training templates are set per group.

---

### 3.6 Tracking Metrics (Coach-Configured)

Beyond lifts, some coaches want to track additional data points in the weekly log. The coach can add custom tracking fields to the weekly logger, such as:

- Sleep quality (1–10)
- Body weight (lbs or kg)
- Sprint time (30m, 60m)
- Jump test results
- Injury notes
- Technique score (coach-rated)

These appear as optional fields in the athlete's weekly submission form.

---

## Part 4: What Stays the Same for Every Coach

Some things are standard across all programmes regardless of configuration:

- Athlete roster management (add, edit, archive)
- 1RM / Max tracking per athlete
- Weekly result submission (mobile app)
- Athlete vs. last week comparison
- Season best tracking
- Squad overview dashboard
- Individual athlete progress reports
- Meet / competition schedule
- Annual plan calendar
- Data privacy and isolation between programmes

These are the non-negotiable core features of the platform.

## Part 5: Commercial Subscription Model

When the app opens to other coaches, pricing could work as follows.

---

### Tier 1 — Solo Coach (Free)
For individual coaches just starting out. This is Coach Vassell's tier.

- 1 coach account
- Up to 30 athletes
- All core features
- NexGen branding visible in the app
- No commercial redistribution

---

### Tier 2 — Club Plan (~$29–$49/month)
For established programmes and clubs.

- Up to 3 coach accounts (head coach + 2 assistants)
- Up to 75 athletes
- All features including custom fields
- Programme can use its own name/branding
- Priority support

---

### Tier 3 — Institution Plan (~$79–$99/month)
For universities, national federations, or large multi-group programmes.

- Unlimited coach accounts
- Unlimited athletes
- All features + API data export
- Custom branding options
- Dedicated onboarding support from NexGen
- First priority for new features

---

### Annual Discount
All paid tiers get 2 months free when billed annually.

## Part 6: How This Changes the Build Plan

Building commercial from day one means a few extra decisions upfront:

**1. Multi-tenant database from the start**
Every database table gets a `programme_id` field. This ensures data isolation from day one and makes it trivial to add new coaches later without restructuring anything.

**2. Coach setup / onboarding flow**
When a new coach signs up, they go through a setup wizard before seeing any athletes:
- Name your programme
- Choose your events
- Choose your lifts
- Set your mesocycle structure
- Set your season start date and phases
- Invite athletes

This wizard is what makes the app feel custom to every coach even though it's the same underlying platform.

**3. Admin dashboard (NexGen internal)**
NexGen needs a backend admin panel to see all programmes, manage subscriptions, handle support issues, and track usage — without ever being able to see private athlete data.

## Part 7: Why This Matters for the Jamaica Market

Coach Vassell coaches athletes from multiple schools and clubs. His network includes coaches at Excelsior, UTech, UWI, and ACE Track Club — all visible in his own spreadsheet's team list.

Each of those coaches has their own athletes, their own training approach, and their own season. If the app is flexible enough for each of them to configure it their way, Coach Vassell can personally introduce them — and every introduction is a potential subscriber.

The Jamaica market is also underserved by tools built for American or European coaching contexts. An app built by a Jamaica-aware team, designed for the Caribbean athletic calendar (ISSA Boys & Girls Champs, JAAA Nationals, Carifta Games), with pricing in a range that works for Jamaican club coaches, has a genuine competitive advantage.
